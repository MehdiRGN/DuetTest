A two-ball reflex game

Split Orbit

  • Hold left or right to spin the pair.
  • Grey walls: turn upright and slip both balls through the gap.
  • Split gates: lie flat, blue over blue, pink over pink. The colors swap sides.
  • Pillars and blocks: find the pose that clears them. Speed shifts every minute.

Keyboard: ← → or A D, Space to start
