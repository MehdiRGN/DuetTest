A two-ball reflex game

Split Orbit

  • Hold left or right to spin the pair.
  • Grey walls: turn upright and slip both balls through the gap.
  • Split gates: lie flat with each ball over its own color. Sides swap.
  • Pillars and spinners: spinners in the middle need you flat, spinners at the sides need you upright.
  • Every minute the speed jumps between ×0.5 and ×2. Every two minutes the ball colors change.

Keyboard: ← → or A D, Space to start, M for music
